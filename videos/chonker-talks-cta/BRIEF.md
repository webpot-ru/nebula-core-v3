## Intent

- workflow: motion-graphics
- flow: automation
- storyboard: no
- category: lower-thirds
- deliverable: transparent overlay
- duration: 3.0 seconds
- resolution: 1920x1080
- frame rate: 30 fps
- audio: none
- language: Russian

## Creative direction

Continue the approved Chonker Talks editorial collage identity: warm torn paper, aubergine ink, oxblood red, cobalt blue and a small acid-yellow accent. The CTA lives entirely in the lower part of the frame so the story artwork remains readable behind it.

The paper strip enters decisively from the left, the channel seal pops in, then the subscription message and stamp land in quick succession. Hold the complete message for about two seconds, give the stamp one restrained pulse, and clear the overlay to the right before the three-second mark.
