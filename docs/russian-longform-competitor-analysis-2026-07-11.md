# Russian Dark-Story Long-Form Competitor Analysis - 2026-07-11

## Decision

`acc1` should be long-form first. Its primary product should be one complete atmospheric story in a 12-30 minute horizontal episode. Shorts should become trailers or isolated source-backed scenes after the long episode exists, not the main format that forces a 4,000-24,000 character story into 2,200 characters.

This is a strategy decision, not audience proof. It requires a small unlisted artifact pilot and then public performance readback.

### Adjacent format competitors

A Russian horror-channel overview distinguishes several useful models: `Nosferatu` uses short fictional provincial horror with matching Russian visual atmosphere; `n3kket` makes researched video essays about lost media, horror games, liminal spaces, and Reddit stories; `Пост Обзор` built an archive of translated `r/nosleep` stories. The same overview describes strong English competitors such as Nexpo as original, highly designed horror essays rather than plain readings: [T-J horror-channel overview](https://t-j.ru/short/cant-sleep/).

The inference is that the market supports both narration and dossier/video-essay formats, but a new channel needs an owned presentation contract. Translation plus a repeated Reddit card is not sufficient differentiation.

## Source-to-duration fit

The committed GitHub source gates found `acc1` `nosleep` candidates between 3,960 and 24,285 characters. At a practical narration pace of roughly 700-900 Russian characters per minute, those sources naturally map to approximately 5-35 minutes before pauses, intro, and sound design.

That source distribution fits long-form materially better than Shorts:

- 3,960-8,000 characters: approximately 6-12 minute episode;
- 8,000-16,000 characters: approximately 12-24 minute episode;
- 16,000-25,000 characters: approximately 24-36 minute episode;
- several proven stories can later form a 45-90 minute compilation.

The duration estimate is a production heuristic, not competitor or audience data.

## Recommended Product Contract

### Primary format

- one complete fictional dark story;
- 12-30 minutes for the first six pilots;
- horizontal 16:9;
- title built around one rule, impossible anomaly, or irreversible decision;
- explicit on-screen and metadata label: fictional story / source adaptation;
- chapters only when the story has natural acts, not arbitrary equal slices;
- original Russian editorial framing and sound design;
- a visual system richer than one static Reddit card.

### Repeatable series

Working series promise: `Одно правило на ночь`.

Episode structure:

1. `0:00-0:20` cold open with the rule/anomaly and a source-backed consequence;
2. `0:20-2:00` minimum setup and narrator identity;
3. middle acts escalate through distinct locations/events;
4. final act delivers the actual source ending;
5. `20-40s` original producer epilogue: what detail makes the story memorable, followed by one discussion question.

### Shorts role

Shorts are discovery assets after the long episode exists:

- one self-contained scene or rule;
- no invented ending;
- point to the complete episode;
- do not publish a summary that destroys the long video's payoff.

## Competitive Advantage We Can Own

1. A strict `one memorable rule` franchise rather than generic scary-story uploads.
2. Clear fiction/unverified labels instead of presenting `nosleep` as real reporting.
3. Distinct scene-based visuals and sound design, not a full-length scrolling/static Reddit card.
4. Source-backed adaptation with preserved ending and no invented facts.
5. Russian narrator performance explicitly locked to AI33/ElevenLabs `eleven_v3`, with auditable request metadata.

## Monetization and Reuse Risk

YouTube says channels consisting of readings of material the creator did not make, mass-produced templates, or low-value slideshows may be ineligible for monetization. Borrowed material needs meaningful original transformation and the channel must still have appropriate rights: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [content monetization guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2490020?hl=en-EN).

Therefore long duration alone is not the product. The required original layer is editorial restructuring, Russian performance, scene direction, sound design, original visual treatment, transparent source/fiction labeling, and preferably author permission or licensed/original submissions for scale.

## Six-Video Pilot

1. Two stories in the 10-16 minute target range.
2. Two stories in the 16-24 minute target range.
3. Two stories in the 24-32 minute target range.
4. Keep all six unlisted until audio, visual variation, fiction labeling, ending preservation, and rights/source notes pass review.
5. Publish sequentially only after the artifact gate passes; do not launch a compilation first.

Measure impressions CTR, first-30-second retention, average view duration, average percentage viewed, watch time, comments, subscribers, and returning viewers. YouTube exposes watch time and average view duration in Engagement analytics and provides video-level key-moment retention after sufficient data: [YouTube Analytics overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9002587?hl=en), [retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en).

## Go / No-Go Rule

- `GO`: at least two of the first six pilots outperform the channel median on CTR, first-30-second retention, average view duration, and comments per 1,000 views.
- `CHANGE`: clicks are healthy but retention falls at the same story stage; repair structure/visual pacing.
- `STOP`: all six have weak CTR and weak first-30-second retention despite two packaging iterations.

No claim of millions of views is justified before this audience readback.
