# Russian Dark-Story Long-Form Competitor Analysis - 2026-07-11

## Decision

`acc1` should be Russian Reddit-horror long-form first, not a literal translated-Reddit reading channel. Its primary product should be one rights-cleared Reddit story transformed into a scene-based Russian episode in the 30-50 minute listening lane. Shorts should become trailers after the full episode exists, and multi-hour compilations should reuse a proven cleared catalog.

Reddit is the required source for every story, but a public post is not permission to translate, adapt, narrate, or monetize it. The stable sourcing and editorial contract is [`russian-horror-editorial-system.md`](russian-horror-editorial-system.md). This is a strategy decision, not audience proof; it still requires unlisted artifact pilots and public performance readback.

### Adjacent format competitors

A Russian horror-channel overview distinguishes several useful models: `Nosferatu` uses short fictional provincial horror with matching Russian visual atmosphere; `n3kket` makes researched video essays about lost media, horror games, liminal spaces, and Reddit stories; `Пост Обзор` built an archive of translated `r/nosleep` stories. The same overview describes strong English competitors such as Nexpo as original, highly designed horror essays rather than plain readings: [T-J horror-channel overview](https://t-j.ru/short/cant-sleep/).

The inference is that the market supports author-written IP, author/dictor partnerships, licensed literary narration, serialized worlds, animation, and compilation listening. Translation plus a repeated Reddit card is not sufficient differentiation.

## Source-to-duration fit

The committed GitHub source gates found `acc1` `nosleep` candidates between 3,017 and 24,285 characters. Measured English word counts map roughly to 4-35 minutes of direct narration. That proves premise supply but also shows why a fixed `2,800+ characters = long-form` rule is inadequate: several eligible candidates are only 4-7 minutes, while the competitor-supported authorial target is materially longer.

Runtime must therefore be measured from the approved Russian narration script rather than inferred from source characters. Thin sources must not be padded, and a licensed source may only be expanded within the granted adaptation rights.

## Recommended Product Contract

### Primary format

- one complete rights-cleared fictional dark story;
- 30-50 minutes for the first six pilots, measured from the approved Russian script;
- horizontal 16:9;
- title built around one rule, impossible anomaly, or irreversible decision;
- explicit on-screen and metadata label: original fiction or licensed adaptation;
- chapters only when the story has natural acts, not arbitrary equal slices;
- a separately approved natural-Russian episode script, not a line-by-line translation;
- original Russian editorial framing and sound design;
- a scene-based visual system; a source card may appear as attribution context but cannot carry the whole episode.

### Repeatable series

Working series promise: `Одно правило на ночь`.

Episode structure:

1. `0:00-0:20` cold open with the rule/anomaly and a source-backed consequence;
2. `0:20-2:00` minimum setup and narrator identity;
3. middle acts escalate through distinct locations/events;
4. final act delivers the actual source ending;
5. `20-40s` original producer epilogue: what detail makes the story memorable, followed by one discussion question.

### Shorts role

Shorts are discovery assets after the long episode exists:

- one self-contained scene or rule;
- no invented ending;
- point to the complete episode;
- do not publish a summary that destroys the long video's payoff.

## Competitive Advantage We Can Own

1. A strict `one memorable rule` franchise rather than generic scary-story uploads.
2. Clear fiction/unverified labels instead of presenting `nosleep` as real reporting.
3. Distinct scene-based visuals and sound design, not a full-length scrolling/static Reddit card.
4. Owned or licensed adaptation with an auditable change ledger and no unapproved invention.
5. Russian narrator performance explicitly locked to AI33/ElevenLabs `eleven_v3`, with auditable request metadata.

## Monetization and Reuse Risk

YouTube says channels consisting of readings of material the creator did not make, mass-produced templates, or low-value slideshows may be ineligible for monetization. Borrowed material needs meaningful original transformation and the channel must still have appropriate rights: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [content monetization guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2490020?hl=en-EN).

Reddit's current terms say users retain ownership of their content, while Reddit receives its own platform license. Reddit's Developer Terms state that user content remains owned by users and that no other rights are implied. Translation and adaptation are derivative uses controlled by the rightsholder: [Reddit User Agreement](https://redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement), [Reddit Developer Terms](https://redditinc.com/policies/developer-terms), [YouTube copyright guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466?hl=en).

Therefore long duration alone is not the product. The required layer is rights clearance, a dedicated Russian script, editorial restructuring, Russian performance, scene direction, sound design, original visual treatment, transparent source/fiction labeling, and independent acceptance. Permission is mandatory for third-party text; it is not merely a preference.

## Six-Video Pilot

1. Build the rights/editorial lane before generating a paid pilot.
2. Produce six standalone stories in the 30-50 minute target lane; shorter exceptions require an unusually strong complete arc rather than padding.
3. Keep all six unlisted until rights, script approval, audio, scene variation, fiction labeling, change ledger, and ending integrity pass review.
4. Publish sequentially only after the artifact gate passes; do not launch a compilation first.

Measure impressions CTR, first-30-second retention, average view duration, average percentage viewed, watch time, comments, subscribers, and returning viewers. YouTube exposes watch time and average view duration in Engagement analytics and provides video-level key-moment retention after sufficient data: [YouTube Analytics overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9002587?hl=en), [retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en).

## Go / No-Go Rule

- `GO`: at least two of the first six pilots outperform the channel median on CTR, first-30-second retention, average view duration, and comments per 1,000 views.
- `CHANGE`: clicks are healthy but retention falls at the same story stage; repair structure/visual pacing.
- `STOP`: all six have weak CTR and weak first-30-second retention despite two packaging iterations.

No claim of millions of views is justified before this audience readback.
